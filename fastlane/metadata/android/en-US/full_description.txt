Scan QR codes with your camera or from a gallery image, then create and share your own.

Scanner
• CameraX and ZXing process QR codes locally.
• Select a QR image with Android Photo Picker.
• Open safe web links.
• Connect or save Wi-Fi through Android confirmation.
• Add contacts and calendar events.
• Open the dialer, Messages, email, and Maps.
• Copy or share any other content.

Creator
• Text, website, Wi-Fi, contact, email, phone, SMS, location, and event QR codes.
• Custom module, corner, and background colors.
• Square, rounded, or dot styles.
• Optional logo from Android Photo Picker.
• Share a PNG through the Android share sheet.

Privacy
• Camera frames stay on your device and are not saved.
• No broad contacts, photo, or storage access.
• No QR history or cloud backend.

Free & Premium
• The free version contains restrained banner ads.
• Full-screen ads appear only after completed scans and no more than once every five minutes.
• Choose monthly Premium or one lifetime purchase through your Google Play account.
• Google consent and ad privacy controls are built in where required.

Requires Android 10 or newer.
