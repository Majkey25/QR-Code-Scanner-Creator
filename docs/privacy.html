Privacy policy

Effective August 24, 2026

QR Code Scanner & Creator works locally on your Android device. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It has no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking SDK, or remote backend.

Camera

Camera permission is used only while the scanner is open. Frames are processed locally to detect QR codes. They are not saved or transmitted.

QR content and device actions

QR content stays in app memory unless you choose an Android action. Android then passes only the relevant content to a system screen or selected app, such as Wi-Fi settings, Contacts, Phone, Messages, email, Maps, Calendar, the clipboard, or the share sheet. Those apps have their own privacy policies.

Contacts, images, and files

The app does not request broad contacts, photo, or storage access. Android's pickers grant access only to the item you choose. Generated QR images use temporary app cache and are shared only when you open the Android share sheet.

Network and retention

Scanning and creation work offline. The app has no QR history or cloud copy. Closing the app clears in-memory content. Android manages temporary cache; uninstalling removes private app data.

Children and changes

This general utility is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect data. Material policy changes will appear here with a new app release.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the public repository.
