Privacy policy

Effective August 24, 2026

QR Code Scanner & Creator keeps scanning and QR creation on your Android device. The app has no developer-operated account system or remote backend.

QR content, camera, and selected images

Camera frames and images selected through Android Photo Picker are decoded locally. They are not saved or transmitted. Scanned and created QR content is not sent to Google Mobile Ads or to the developer.

When you choose an Android action, only the relevant content is passed to the system screen or app you select. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Contacts and files

The app does not request broad contacts, photo, or storage access. Android's pickers grant access only to the item you choose. Generated QR images use temporary app cache and are shared only when you open the Android share sheet.

Advertising

The free version uses Google Mobile Ads. After any required consent choice, Google's SDK may automatically collect and share the device IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, Android advertising ID, app set ID, and related device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Google encrypts this data in transit. Ad requests never include QR content.

Users in supported regions receive Google's consent form before ads can be requested. When required, Ad privacy options in About lets you change that choice. QR Premium disables all ad requests after Google Play verifies the entitlement.

See Google's advertising privacy information and Google's privacy policy.

QR Premium purchases

QR Premium is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. The app queries Google Play for the product and current purchase state, and receives the product ID, purchase status, and purchase token needed to unlock and acknowledge Premium. Payment details are handled by Google and are not available to the developer. The app has no purchase backend.

Local storage and retention

The app has no QR history or cloud copy. It stores only a local completed-scan counter and last-ad timestamp to limit full-screen ad frequency. Android manages temporary cache. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes local data. Google and any app you choose to open may retain data under their own policies.

Children and changes

This general utility is not directed to children. Material policy changes will appear here with a new app release.
