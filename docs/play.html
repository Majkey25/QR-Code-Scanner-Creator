Privacy policy

Scanning, selected content and sharing

QR scanning and creation run on your Android device. The app has no developer-operated account system or remote backend. Camera frames and images selected through Android Photo Picker are decoded locally and are not saved or transmitted. Scanned and created QR content is not sent to Google Mobile Ads or to the developer.

The app does not request broad contacts, photo or storage access. Android's pickers grant access only to the item you select. When you choose an Android action or sharing destination, only the relevant content is passed to the system screen or app you select. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Advertising and consent

The free app uses Google Mobile Ads. After any required consent choice, Google's SDK may automatically collect and share the device IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information, Android advertising ID, app set ID, and related device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google encrypts this data in transit. Ad requests never include QR content.

Google's User Messaging Platform supplies the required consent form. The app checks Google's consent status before requesting ads. When required, Ad privacy options in About lets you change that choice. Premium disables ad requests after Google Play verifies the entitlement.

See Google's advertising privacy information and Google's privacy policy.

Google Play purchases

Google Play handles Premium payments. The app queries the available products and current purchases, receiving the product ID, purchase status and purchase token needed to unlock and acknowledge Premium. The developer does not receive payment details and operates no purchase backend. Pending purchases do not activate Premium.

Local storage and retention

The app has no QR history or developer-operated cloud copy. Each shared PNG uses a separate file in private app cache. Before another share, the app removes cached share files older than 24 hours and limits previous files to seven, then creates the next file. Up to eight files are retained. This cleanup runs only when another share is requested, not on a timer. Without another share, files may remain until Android reclaims cache, you clear app cache or data, or uninstall. Copies saved by a sharing destination are unaffected.

Google's advertising and consent components manage their own local preferences and caches. Versions before 1.1.7 recorded scan counters and ad timestamps to limit full-screen ad frequency; current versions do not. Clearing app data can remove values left by those older versions. Clearing app data or uninstalling does not cancel subscriptions or remove copies held by Google or a sharing destination.

Terms and conditions

The app scans and creates QR codes on Android 10 and newer. Use only content you are allowed to process. Check the destination before opening a link, importing a contact, connecting to Wi-Fi or sharing a code. Recognizing a QR code does not prove its destination is safe. Generated QR readability depends on content, size, contrast, logos and the receiving scanner. Test important codes before printing or distributing them.

Scanning and QR creation are available without Premium. The free app includes ads. Premium removes ads and belongs to the Google Play account that purchases it. Monthly Premium renews automatically at the price shown in the app and Google Play until canceled. Lifetime Premium is a one-time unlock for this app, not a recurring charge or a purchase of other applications. Review the current price and benefits before confirming a purchase.

Third-party apps and services operate under their own terms.
